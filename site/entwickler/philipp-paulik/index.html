Philipp Paulik

Philipp Paulik is an AI pioneer and strategist, web designer, UI/UX designer, software engineer and author. He develops AI strategies and builds his own agents and tools, which he releases as open source and as npm packages, including true-code, ZWEP, Flower and the web design studio Somnia.

He is the creator and developer of true-code, a free and open-source (MIT) agentic coding harness written in Rust, and the provider of this site (see the legal notice).
